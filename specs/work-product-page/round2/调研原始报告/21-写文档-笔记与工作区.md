## 1. Notion: Notion AI, Notion Agent and Custom Agents

1. **What it is.** A team wiki, docs and databases tool that is now positioned as a workspace for agents.
2. **How the AI works.**
   - **Select-text menu: yes.** Highlight text, then "Edit with AI"; you can keep, reject or retry the result. https://www.notion.com/help/guides/notion-ai-for-docs [F]
   - **Notion Agent writes for real.** It creates and edits pages and databases, including views and relations. It *cannot* create automations, buttons, formulas, comments or reminders, and cannot change permissions. https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent [F]
   - **Suggested edits** shipped 2026-08-28: agents "propose changes instead of making them directly." https://www.notion.com/releases [F]
   - **Custom Agents** run on triggers: schedules, database or comment events, Slack messages, or a finished AI Meeting Note. They can hand work to sub-agents. https://www.notion.com/help/custom-agents [F]
3. **Where it can act.**
   - Slack: posts and replies as you. Gmail: drafts and sends, with confirmation. Calendar: creates and edits events. https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent [F]
   - Notion 3.8 (2026-10-13) lets agents read from and act in 150+ outside tools through MCP. It also has its own MCP server that external agents can read and write through, plus a Skills API. https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-10-13 [F]
   - The official MCP server lets external clients create pages and databases, using OAuth. https://developers.notion.com/docs/mcp [F]
4. **Outputs and sources.** Outputs are real pages and database rows. The "Find" feature advertises answers with citations; the Agent help page doesn't explain how citations appear. 3.8 adds version history with edit attribution. [F, same links]
5. **Memory and models.**
   - A personal instructions page ("My Notion AI") that the agent can update itself, plus Skills. [F notion-agent]
   - Model picker covers Claude, GPT, Gemini and Grok; premium models cost credits, and admins control which models are allowed (2026-09-09). [F]
   - A ChatGPT Plus/Pro subscription can now pay for Notion AI usage (Business and Enterprise only). [F 3.8]
   - Memory lives in Notion pages, so it carries across model switches. This is my inference.
6. **Building your own.** No-code Custom Agents. "Workers" (beta) run custom code. [F pricing]
7. **Local vs cloud.** Cloud. Offline mode is per page and per device, and only the first 50 rows of a database download. https://www.notion.com/help/guides/working-offline-in-notion-everything-you-need-to-know [F] AI does not work offline [S 9to5mac/maketecheasier]. Zero data retention with model providers is Enterprise-only. https://www.notion.com/pricing [F]
8. **Pricing and status.**
   - AI comes only with Business and Enterprise. Free and Plus get a "limited trial." [F pricing]
   - Business is $20 per member per month billed annually, $24 monthly. https://www.breeze.pm/articles/notion-ai-pricing [F, third-party]
   - Custom Agents cost $10 per 1,000 credits. They became paid and generally available on 2026-05-04 [S].
   - The personal Agent runs on a usage allowance measured over 6-hour and monthly windows. https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-usage-allowance-for-notion-ai [F]
9. **Complaints.**
   - Unpredictable credit costs: one heavy user's workspace spent about $337 in a month. https://matthiasfrank.de/en/notion-agent-pricing/ [F]
   - Capterra reviewers want to opt out of AI and say the trial credits run out fast, as quoted via a competitor's review. https://eesel.ai/blog/notion-ai-review [F, secondary, competitor-authored]
   - Its May 2026 claim that connectors are "read-only" is now outdated.
   - A claimed r/Notion "TERRIBLE" pricing thread and a claimed "7,000 credits ≈ $70" review **could not be verified**. The hunted.space page I fetched did not contain the quote.
10. **Overlap and lead.**
   - Closest overlap: an assistant that performs the same actions as the UI, proposed edits, select-text edits, and MCP in both directions.
   - Clearly ahead: event-triggered agents, Slack/Gmail/Calendar actions, 150+ MCP connections, admin controls, and mobile.

## 3. Craft (Craft Docs, Craft Agents, MCP)

1. **What it is.** Polished docs and notes, Apple-first, for individuals and small teams.
2. **How the AI works.**
   - The Assistant has two modes. **Explore** proposes changes for approval; **Execute** applies them directly.
   - Execute can create subpages, documents and folders, move content and delete it.
   - ⌘Z undoes the Assistant's whole last response as one step.
   - Execute works on macOS and iOS only; on Windows and Web it is "coming later." https://support.craft.do/en/ai-assistant/editing.md [F]
   - Select blocks then ⌘↵ was the documented entry point in 2022 (https://craft.do/blog/craft-ai-assistant [F, old]). The current select-text menu is unverified.
3. **Where it can act.**
   - The MCP server gives read access to selected docs by default. "Full space access" (v3.3.5+) adds create, update and delete for documents, tasks and collections. Clients include Claude, a ChatGPT plugin, Cursor and Raycast. https://support.craft.do/en/integrate/mcp [F]
   - **Craft Agents** is a separate open-source desktop agent app (Apache-2.0, about 7.3k stars). It works with Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter and Ollama. It connects to MCP servers, REST APIs (Google, Slack, Microsoft) and local files. Permission modes are safe, ask and allow-all. https://github.com/lukilabs/craft-agents-oss [F]
4. **Outputs and sources.** Outputs are real docs. Citations are not documented.
5. **Memory and models.**
   - Cloud models: Max (Claude Sonnet 4.6), Fast (Claude Haiku 4.5), Core (GPT-5 Nano).
   - **On-device:** Apple Foundation Model and Llama 3.2, read-only. https://support.craft.do/en/ai-assistant/models.md [F]
   - Bring-your-own-key works with OpenAI and Anthropic keys. https://support.craft.do/en/ai-assistant/usage.md [F]
   - Sessions sync across devices. No memory feature is documented.
6. **Building your own.** Plain-language background workflows were *announced* 2025-11-27; current status is unknown. https://www.craft.do/blog/your-content-is-yours [F]
7. **Local vs cloud.** Cloud sync. On-device models send "no data" to servers. [F] SOC 2 [S].
8. **Pricing.**
   - Free: 15 lifetime credits. Plus: 50 per month. https://www.craft.do/pricing [F] The page showed JPY only.
   - Top-ups: $10 for up to 300 sessions, $40 for 1,500, $100 for 4,300. [F usage]
   - Craft says it will not put AI behind a higher pricing tier. [F blog]
9. **Complaints.**
   - App Store review "Bafflingly Buggy": Mac "crashes at least once a day." Another review: "$95/year?" https://apps.apple.com/us/app/craft-docs-notes-tasks-ai/id1487937127?see-all=reviews [F]
   - No Android app, and no end-to-end encryption [S].
   - I found no AI-specific complaints.
10. **Overlap and lead.**
   - Overlap: proposed versus applied edits with one-step undo, MCP, an open-source agent app.
   - Ahead: polish, on-device models, an iOS editing agent.

## 5. Anytype

1. **What it is.** A local-first, end-to-end encrypted object workspace for privacy-minded users.
2. **How the AI works.** There is **no generally available built-in AI**.
   - An alpha agent, codename "Bobrik," was announced 2026-05-28. It creates and bulk-edits objects and builds mini-apps.
   - It runs Claude Sonnet through Anthropic's API on Anytype's servers, and the internal team reviews alpha conversations. Access is via waitlist. https://community.anytype.io/t/ai-agent-on-anytype-alpha-demo/30881 [F]
3. **Where it can act.**
   - The Local API runs on localhost from the desktop app and works offline. https://doc.anytype.io/anytype/features/integrations/local-api.md [F]
   - Official MCP server, read and write (MIT licence, 533 stars). https://github.com/anyproto/anytype-mcp [F]
   - An "Agents' Skill" gives external agents a sandboxed JS runtime. https://doc.anytype.io/anytype/features/integrations/anytype-agents-skill.md [F]
4. **Outputs.** Real objects, through the API.
5. **Models.** External agents bring their own. Local models for the built-in agent are only a stated aim. [F]
6. **Building your own.** The API, the Skill and mini-apps (alpha).
7. **Local vs cloud.** Keys are generated on the device and never sent. Backup nodes cannot read content. Local-only mode and self-hosting are available. https://doc.anytype.io/anytype/data/privacy-and-encryption.md [F]
8. **Pricing.** Not retrievable: the pricing page did not render, and the memberships doc lists no prices. [F]
9. **Complaints.**
   - Threads: "Should a privacy-focused note-taking app adopt AI?" (28 replies), "Roadmap for local AI/LLM integration?" (23 replies), and MCP connection and 500-error bugs. https://community.anytype.io/tag/ai [F]

10. **Overlap and lead.**
   - Overlap: local-first data, external agents through MCP.
   - Ahead: encryption, sync and self-hosting, plus a large existing user base.

## Patterns across products

- **AI that writes into real things is now standard.** Notion, Coda, Craft, Tana, Capacities, Heptabase and Mem all create or edit pages, rows or objects.
  - Most of them gate writes behind review: Notion suggested edits, Craft Explore/Execute plus ⌘Z, Tana proposals, Capacities approve or auto-approve.
  - Molis Work's undoable action cards are table stakes, not a differentiator.
- **Every app now exposes itself as an MCP server.** All eleven offer one in some form: Notion, Coda, Craft, Tana, Capacities, Heptabase, Mem, Granola, Reflect Classic, Anytype, and Obsidian through community servers.
  - Almost all are hosted with OAuth. Local servers: Anytype, Tana Outliner, Obsidian community servers.
  - Granola's is read-only.
- **Acting across apps (as an MCP client) is rare.** Notion (150+ connections; Slack, Gmail and Calendar actions), Tana (Slack, GitHub, Linear and others, gated by approval) and Craft Agents do it. Others act only inside their own app.
- **Select-text menus are common:** Notion, Capacities, Reflect, Copilot for Obsidian, and Craft in older docs. None of them offers next actions from *any* plugin. That cross-plugin context bar is a real gap Molis Work could fill.
- **Citations:** strong in Heptabase (paragraphs and timestamps), Granola, Reflect, and Notion Find. Undocumented in Coda, Craft, Mem and Tana.
- **Local and bring-your-own-key:**
  - Truly local: Obsidian, Reflect Open, Anytype.
  - On-device models: Craft (Apple models).
  - Bring-your-own-key: Heptabase (public-HTTPS endpoints only), Craft, Reflect, and Obsidian plugins.
  - Notion now accepts a ChatGPT subscription as payment.
  - None offers the combination of a local-first, bring-your-own-key assistant that acts across plugins.
- **Common complaints:**
  - Unpredictable credit pricing and fast-draining AI budgets (Notion, Coda, Heptabase Pro, Mem's caps on new notes).
  - Privacy-minded users resisting cloud AI (Anytype threads, Capterra users wanting to opt out).
  - The do-it-yourself setup burden of Obsidian plus MCP.
  - Performance and bugs (Craft Mac, Heptabase).
- **Watch closely:** Reflect Open is the nearest philosophical twin to Molis Work. Notion 3.8 is the nearest functional twin. Anytype's alpha agent is the nearest twin for privacy-focused users.
