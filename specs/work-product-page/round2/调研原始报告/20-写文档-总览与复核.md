# Molis Work for non-coders: competitive research and positioning (as of 2026-10-16)

## How this was researched, and how far to trust it

- **Method.** Four research passes ran in parallel: workspace and notes apps, big platforms, the Chinese market, and automation and app builders. I then re-checked the claims that carry the argument myself, and read the molis-work repository through the GitHub API.
- **Confidence tags.** **[F]** means the page was fetched and read this session (2026-10-16). **[S]** means the claim rests only on a search snippet; treat it as weaker evidence.
- **Search gaps.**
  - The shared web-search budget (200 calls) ran out partway through. Later checks used direct fetches of official URLs only.
  - Reddit, G2, Capterra, openai.com, help.openai.com and chatgpt.com were blocked or refused.
  - Complaint evidence therefore comes from official forums, Trustpilot, Product Hunt, App Store reviews and news.
  - Coverage of Chinese user complaints (少数派 / 知乎 / 即刻) is thin.
  - Items marked UNVERIFIED must not be used on the site.
- **What I re-fetched myself:** Menlo 2026; both QuestMobile reports; Notion 3.8; the Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes; Claude for Google Workspace; the Raycast changelog; Reflect Open; the Sign in with ChatGPT docs; Anthropic's authentication policy; the Claude and Gemini memory-import pages; LayerX (via The Register); the CJR citation study; Apple's macOS Tahoe press release; Anthropic Desktop Extensions; Simon Willison's "lethal trifecta" post; Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026; Pew 2025; and the molis-work repo.
- **Weaker sourcing for ChatGPT.** ChatGPT's 2026 product names (Pages, Dots, Work, plugins) come from a mirror of OpenAI's release notes, because openai.com was blocked. Treat them as moderate confidence.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Most of the pains in the draft "Why Molis Work?" are already being addressed by shipping products in 2026.**
   - **AI that edits inside the document, with approval cards:**
     - Copilot Agent Mode, the default since 2026-04-22.
     - Claude for Google Workspace, beta since 2026-10-06; it sees your selection.
     - ChatGPT for Word, 2026-09-17, on every plan including Free.
   - **Agents that work across apps:** Copilot Cowork (GA 2026-06-16), Notion 3.8 with 150+ MCP connections (2026-10-13), Feishu 8.0, 豆包工作 and Tencent WorkBuddy.
   - **Memory import between assistants:** Claude and Gemini both added it in March 2026.
   - **MCP servers** now exist on almost every notes app.
   - So "AI acts on your real things, with your approval" is table stakes, not a differentiator.
2. **Still unoccupied: one product that combines four things.** Each piece exists somewhere; no major product has all four.
   - Local storage on your Mac, with a published list of every outbound request.
   - Bring your own model, including DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM and MiniMax, with no credit markup.
   - One action catalog that the UI, the assistant, workflows and outside agents all call, granted per action and per client.
   - Selecting text offers a next action that lands in a *different* tool (a destination), not only a rewrite.
3. **The closest threats:**
   - **Raycast:** Mac-native, sees the focused window or element, runs agents, works over MCP, accepts your own key or your own subscription, and supports Ollama. It ships today.
   - **Reflect Open:** MIT-licensed, local Markdown, your own key, answers with citations, select-text actions.
   - **Claude Cowork and its Workspace add-ins:** the closest overall functional overlap.
   - **Notion 3.8.**
   - **Tencent WorkBuddy in China:** 6.58M monthly active users, works in local folders, skills, MCP, approval gates.
4. **"Continue the same page with a different AI" is not a demonstrated need for this audience.**
   - People use a rotation of about 3 assistants, chosen by task.
   - They do move between vendors, which is why both Anthropic and Google built import tools.
   - I found no evidence that non-coders alternate models within one piece of work.
   - Reframe this as portability: the work lives with you, not inside one vendor's chat.
5. **The biggest gap for non-coders is access, not features.**
   - There is no public signed installer.
   - The public README speaks to coding-agent users (Codex, Claude Code, terminals).
   - Asking non-coders for an API key is a real barrier. A new route exists: OpenAI's "Sign in with ChatGPT" plan usage has been open to open-source local clients without approval since 2026-09-29.

## D. Does "continue the same page with a different AI" matter to this audience?

**Mostly no, not as model-switching within a task.** The evidence:

- **People do use several assistants.** About 3.0 assistants per AI user in a "rotation" (Menlo 2026, n=5,067, fielded July 2026). But Menlo does not measure switching within the same task. Menlo 2025 found most people reach for one favourite tool for nearly every job. https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-consumer-ai/ [F]
- **In China, switching happens by scenario, not by turn** (QuestMobile).
- **Model menus exist but are niche:**
  - Microsoft offers "Switch Model" as a way to *regenerate an alternative answer* (2026-10-06), not as a way of working.
  - The one forum complaint about losing in-chat switching comes from a Plus power user.
  - Model menus are standard in Chinese products (Feishu lists 5 vendors, WorkBuddy 4, ima switches per step), so offering one is parity, not an advantage.
- **What is clearly real is moving between vendors and avoiding lock-in.** Both Anthropic and Google built import tools in March 2026, and every vendor keeps memory inside its own walls.

**Recommendation.** Drop "continue with a different AI" as a headline benefit. Replace it with **"your pages, sources and history are on your Mac; whichever AI you use now or next year picks up from there"**, plus **"use the AI you already have"**.

That second claim is only partly true today:
- **Any API key** (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, OpenRouter, compatible endpoints) works. Verified in the repo: `apps/local-host/src/model-provider-templates.ts`.
- **An unmodified Claude Code signed in with the user's own subscription** is permitted by Anthropic's policy and matches the README's Claude Code runtime recipe. https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance [F]
- **ChatGPT subscriptions are possible but not built.** Plan usage is open to open-source local clients without approval (Plus and Pro, preview limits). It goes through the Responses API, and Molis currently uses chat-completions or messages formats. https://developers.openai.com/siwc/quickstart [F], https://flaviocopes.com/sign-in-with-chatgpt/ [F]
- **A Claude subscription inside Molis's own assistant is not allowed** under the same Anthropic policy.

## E. Positioning

### E1. Advantages Molis Work can defend, each with proof and a condition

1. **Your work stays on your Mac, and every outbound request is listed.**
   - Content lives in local SQLite. The repo's `docs/platform/NETWORK.en.md` lists every class of outbound request: models, connectors, AnySearch, the plugin build check, and so on.
   - Rare: no competitor in this scan publishes an equivalent ledger.
   - Condition: phrase it as "stored locally", not "never leaves".
2. **Open source (MIT) and no lock-in.**
   - Verifiable (repo created 2026-08-15, MIT).
   - Do not imply a community: the repo has 5 stars.
3. **Bring your own model, including Chinese providers, with no credit markup.**
   - A direct answer to the credit complaints in section C (P8).
   - Condition: say users pay their provider directly.
4. **One set of actions for you, the assistant, workflows and outside agents, each granted individually.**
   - `docs/mcp.en.md`: an action appears to an outside client only after you grant that exact action, version and scope; revoking takes effect on the next call.
   - That is finer-grained than Notion's revoke-per-connection or Granola's read-only switch.
5. **Select a line and send it where it belongs.**
   - Next actions that land in *another* plugin, not just a rewrite.
   - Irreversible actions are never offered (comment in `packages/kernel/src/contextual.ts`).
   - Distinct from Apple Writing Tools, Word and Notion menus. Raycast is close but prompt-driven.
6. **Every assistant action is a card you can undo.**
   - Stronger than Cowork ("no undo") or Microsoft (version history).
   - Similar to Craft (⌘Z per response) and Capacities (undo until the next message).
   - Condition: true only for actions that are actually reversible. A sent Gmail message is not.

### E2. Claims Molis Work must not make

- **"Select text anywhere on your Mac" or "AI across all your apps."**
  - Molis Work's bar works inside Molis Work.
  - System-wide selection is Molis Tool, which is unreleased.
  - Apple, Raycast and Click to Do already do it system-wide.
- **"Your data never leaves your computer" or "private AI".**
  - Selected material goes to the configured model provider.
  - Web search goes to `api.anysearch.com`.
  - Functions call `api.typesafe.ai`.
  - The optional OAuth broker is `auth.molis.ai`.
  - The plugin build check sends package names to npm.
  - All of these are documented in NETWORK.en.md.
- **"Works with local or offline models."** There is no Ollama template. The OpenAI-compatible option might work against a local endpoint, but that is untested.
- **"Download now", "free" or "no subscription".**
  - There is no signed installer: the v0.2.0 release has no assets, and its notes say a public DMG is pending signing and notarization.
  - The README's "Try it in 3 minutes: Download the DMG" contradicts its own "Product boundaries" section. Fix the README.
- **"The only tool where AI acts with your approval", "MCP-enabled", or "AI that does the work for you".** These are now common.
- **"Switch AIs mid-page" as a key benefit.**
- **"Use your Claude subscription"** inside Molis's own assistant.
- **"Safe" AI-built plugins.** Say "previewed, sandboxed and checked before install".
- **"For teams" or "collaborative".** Molis Work is single-user and local, with no sharing or mobile app.
- **"Better than Notion, Copilot or Feishu" in general.** Molis is behind on PPT, forms and datasets, calendar, meeting capture, Office-format fidelity, integration count, mobile and admin controls.

### E3. Gaps and ideas to feed back to the team

1. **Access first.**
   - Ship a notarized installer.
   - Rewrite the README and onboarding for non-coders. Today they lead with Codex, Claude Code, terminals and LaunchAgent.
   - Make first-run work without a terminal.
2. **Sign in with ChatGPT plan usage.** Removes the API-key barrier for Plus and Pro users; open-source local clients are eligible without approval. Needs Responses API support.
3. **Expose Molis actions as macOS App Intents and a Services or Share entry.** Users could then call "add to todo" or "send to draft" from Spotlight, Shortcuts, Siri or any app. This is where the operating system is heading, and it bridges to Molis Tool.
4. **Readable files.** Mirror Pages and notes as Markdown, and export to .docx, Google Docs and Feishu. This strengthens the no-lock-in claim (Reflect Open and Obsidian set that bar).
5. **Provenance that survives.** Keep source links at paragraph level through edits and into todos and decks. Mark AI-created content, as Heptabase does with "Created by AI".
6. **A per-action cost readout** (tokens and money), because bringing your own key moves the bill onto the user.
7. **Guard against prompt injection.** Treat Feed and web content as untrusted, and require approval for any outbound action (Gmail send, MCP writes) in a session that read untrusted content.
8. **Support local models properly** with an Ollama or LM Studio template, and label which features send data out.
9. **For China:** write back into Feishu, DingTalk and WPS documents (a lark-mcp connector already exists); import WeChat articles (ima's strength); default the model setup to DeepSeek or Qwen.
10. **Mobile capture**, at least for 灵光 and Inbox. Readwise, Mem and Granola all have it.

**Competitors to watch:** Raycast, Reflect Open, Claude Cowork and its add-ins, Notion 3.8, Tencent WorkBuddy, Craft Agents (open source), Anytype's agent.
