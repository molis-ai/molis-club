## 报告

All paths below are relative to REPO=/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-creamiex-molis-ai-molis-club/317c4333-f56b-4333-a796-3a9c88c3d9b3/scratchpad/molis-work-src/repo (HEAD 19dc13d4, 2026-10-16). "WB" means REPO/apps/workbench/src.

**What v0.2.0 looked like (9f0d5191, 2026-09-11).** The UI was still branded GoalBoard (strings like 开始使用 GoalBoard). It had only 4 native plugins: artifacts, feed, goals, work. It had a left navigator: `goals-page-renderer.ts` at v0.2.0 renders `desktop-module-list` "工作台目录" and a `personal-sidebar-footer`. Tabs were Goal-only (`scripts/work-tabs.ts`, `desktop-work-tabs`). v0.2.0 had none of these files: tab-workspace, side-panel*, context-*, assistant-*, global-search, project-home, settings-sections, settings-memory, `horizontal/{memory,search}`, `apps/local-host/src/computer`. `docs/releases/CHANGELOG.md` §Unreleased (post-v0.2.0) lists them as new (#96 search, #98 系统助理, #118 侧栏, #119 情境, #130 记忆, #147 首页, #159 到达, #171 切换器, #140 bell).

1. **Tabs + split panes: real. Not at v0.2.0.**
   - `WB/scripts/client/tab-workspace.ts:36` says "Cross-plugin tabs and split panes". The pure rules are in `WB/tab-workspace-ops.ts:2` ("additive tabs, user groups, and split panes"); helpers are `tab-split-drop.ts`, `tab-reorder.ts` and `tab-strip-share.ts`.
   - Each pane has its own `tab-strip` (:347, with drop edges 拆到左侧/右侧/上方/下方).
   - Tab menu (:1447–1455): 固定标签/取消固定标签, 添加到新分组, 添加到分组, 从分组移除, 关闭标签. Groups have a name and 8 colours. Layout menu 布局与分屏 (:1523): 向右/向下/向左/向上分屏, 关闭当前窗口.
   - Location chip and cover chip; cover names at :360.

2. **Left sidebar: none now.**
   - `DESIGN.md:196`: "There is no global left sidebar."
   - Directories are columns inside a plugin's sheet: `renderDirectoryPluginSections` in `WB/immersive-shell.ts`, toggled by 收起目录/展开目录 (`WB/settings-navigation.ts:66`, `WB/scripts/client/documents-state.ts:107`). On phones they become a drawer (`goals-page-renderer.ts:287`).
   - v0.2.0 did have the left navigator described above.

3. **Bottom bar: real. Not at v0.2.0.**
   - `renderWorkbenchBar` in `WB/immersive-shell.ts:246–273` (aria 底栏).
   - Left: the plugin switcher "全部插件与 Dock". Its head has 搜索 ⌘K, 插件市场 and 插件创作工作台. Then `dock-pins` 常驻插件, starting with 项目首页.
   - Centre: the Assistant composer.
   - Right: residents `BAR_RESIDENT_IDS = ["shelf","lingguang"]` (:105) plus the button labelled "侧栏：讨论、浏览器与文件". The 讨论 button is this side-panel toggle; it has no separate label.
   - Project button/menu: `settings-navigation.ts:48` (切换项目, 管理项目, …, 项目设置, 能力, 设置). The +N overflow is `[data-dock-more]`.

4. **Side panel (侧栏): real. Not at v0.2.0.**
   - `WB/side-panel.ts:34–56` has tabs 讨论 · 浏览器 · 电脑 · 文件 plus plugin side views. Those come from manifests with `slot:"side"`: 灵光 (lingguang), Issue capture (issues, ⌘⇧I) and Pages 评论与备注.
   - Browser (`side-panel-browser.ts:4`): a screencast of the local Host's browser with address bar, 把这一页交给助理 and 助理在网站上的权限.
   - **Computer use is real but partial.** `side-panel-computer.ts:4–9`: the Assistant operates macOS apps through the Host's helper. The tab shows the target app's window live (password fields covered), with 暂停 / 交还助理 / 接手 / 放进 Shelf / 停止 and a per-step approval.
   - Backend: `apps/local-host/src/computer/*` (driver, policy, app-tiers, records) and a Swift helper in `apps/local-host/native/computer/Sources/{main,Input,Observe,Presence}.swift`.
   - `specs/computer-use/spec.md:3` status is "进行中（2026-10-11）". A minimal real-device slice is proven; the MiniMax-M3 model drove TextEdit and Calculator (29 steps). It is macOS 14+ only.

5. **Context toolbar (情境浮条, `.mw-ctx`): real. Not at v0.2.0.**
   - `packages/design-system/src/context-toolbar.ts:7–8`: the plugin's fixed tools on the left, then at most three recommended actions, 更多, and ✦助理. The ranking basis is labelled 按规则 or Jev (`context-bar.ts`).
   - It appears after a selection is steady for 350ms. ⌘. summons it while writing (`context-bar.ts:100`).
   - Pages publishes `molis:surface-focus` itself (`plugins/native/pages/src/client.ts:300`) and is the only plugin with tools (format bar merged in).
   - A generic DOM fallback (`WB/scripts/client/context-actions.ts:82–108`) gives actions-only toolbars on text selections inside any `[data-assistant-context]` element. That covers alchemist, coding, cognia, dataset, files, form, images, jelly, lingguang, ppt, schedule, shelf, todo, workflows and the feed view (`navigation-feed.ts`).
   - Spec status: `specs/context-toolbar/spec.md:3` is 执行中; W1/W2 merged (#411, #494) per `specs/context-program/spec.md:7`.

6. **Assistant (助理): real. Not at v0.2.0.**
